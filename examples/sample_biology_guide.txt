1. OVERVIEW OF PHOTOSYNTHESIS
Photosynthesis is the fundamental biological process by which autotrophic organisms, 
including plants, algae, and cyanobacteria, convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose. 
The general chemical equation for photosynthesis is:
6 CO2 + 6 H2O + Light Energy ---> C6H12O6 + 6 O2

Photosynthesis takes place within specialized double-membrane organelles called chloroplasts. 
Chloroplasts contain thylakoid membranes stacked into grana, surrounded by a fluid region called the stroma.

2. LIGHT-DEPENDENT REACTIONS
The light-dependent reactions occur within the thylakoid membrane. Chlorophyll a and chlorophyll b 
absorb photons primarily in the blue and red wavelengths while reflecting green light. 
When light strikes Photosystem II, water molecules are split (photolysis), releasing oxygen gas (O2) 
as a byproduct and producing hydrogen ions and high-energy electrons. 

These electrons travel along the electron transport chain (ETC) to Photosystem I, generating ATP via 
chemiosmosis and reducing NADP+ to NADPH.

3. THE CALVIN CYCLE (LIGHT-INDEPENDENT REACTIONS)
The Calvin Cycle takes place in the stroma of the chloroplast. It does not require direct sunlight 
but relies on the ATP and NADPH produced by the light-dependent reactions. 
The enzyme RuBisCO catalyzes carbon fixation, attaching carbon dioxide (CO2) to ribulose 1,5-bisphosphate (RuBP). 
Through a series of enzymatic reductions, G3P (glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate) is synthesized, which is subsequently 
used to form glucose and other essential carbohydrates.

4. FACTORS AFFECTING PHOTOSYNTHETIC RATE
The rate of photosynthesis is influenced by three main environmental factors:
a) Light Intensity: As light intensity increases, the photosynthetic rate rises until reaching a saturation point.
b) Carbon Dioxide Concentration: Higher CO2 availability speeds up carbon fixation in the Calvin Cycle.
c) Temperature: Enzymes like RuBisCO function optimally between 20°C and 35°C. Excessive heat denatures enzymes.

5. GLOSSARY & KEY TERMS
- Chlorophyll: The primary green pigment responsible for absorbing light energy.
- Stroma: Fluid-filled space surrounding thylakoids inside the chloroplast.
- Photolysis: The splitting of water molecules using light energy.
- RuBisCO: The enzyme responsible for fixing carbon dioxide in the Calvin Cycle.
